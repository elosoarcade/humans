# Humans
**Humans MCBE Add-On adds humans to your world or server, making the experience less lonely.**

These humans spawn randomly all over the overworld. They come in 11 different designs and can be named. They can also be "hired" by offering them a highly variable amount of emeralds. Once hired, they follow and protect you from any monster except the Warden and piglins, to avoid unnecessary and potentially lethal fights.

If you don't want to hire them, then equip an emerald and they'll follow you, so you can take them into battle. They are very strong and resistant, but if you consider that they have been hurt, you can heal them with cookies and apples. They are also equipped with their own copper sword, but if you drop better swords, they will pick them up.

*   ❤️ Health: 100-150
*   ⚔️ Attack: 3-6

**NOTE:** More hand textures will be fixed and new ones added in future updates.
