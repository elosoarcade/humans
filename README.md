# Humans

**Humans MCBE Add-On adds humans to your world or server, making the experience less lonely.**

These humans spawn randomly all over the overworld. They come in many different designs and can be named. They can also be "hired" by offering them a highly variable amount of emeralds. Once hired, they follow and protect you from any monster except the Warden and piglins, to avoid unnecessary and potentially lethal fights.

If you don't want to hire them, then equip an emerald and they'll follow you, so you can take them into battle. They are very strong and resistant, but if you consider that they have been hurt, you can heal them with cookies and apples. Humans are also equipped with their own copper sword or axe, but if you drop a better one, they will pick them up.

*   ❤️ Health: 100-150
*   ⚔️ Attack: 3-6

**NOTE:** More textures will be added in future updates.
